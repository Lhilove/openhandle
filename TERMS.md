## The service

OpenHandle maps a handle to a public wallet address. OpenHandle is **not** a
wallet, custodian, money transmitter, exchange or broker. It never holds funds
and never handles private keys or seed phrases.

## Your responsibilities

- You are responsible for your account, your wallet and every transaction you
  sign.
- Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible. Confirm the recipient,
  network, asset and amount before signing.
- Network fees may apply and are paid by the sender's wallet. OpenHandle does not
  claim that payments are free.
- Do not use OpenHandle to impersonate others, to commit fraud, or to violate any
  law.

## Handles

Handles are unique and immutable in this release. A handle is not a trademark and
carries no claim of trademark clearance. We may reclaim handles that violate
these terms or impersonate others.

## Verification

"Wallet verified" means a signature proved control of an address. "Identity
verified" means a provider's official API confirmed control of a social account.
Neither is legal or government identity verification.
